The Journey & Experience

Emily Cohn was a five-year varsity tennis starter at Mount Carmel Academy in New Orleans, Louisiana, and was elected team captain for her senior season. Over five years of rigorous competition, tennis became much more than a sport—it served as a foundational platform for learning how to lead, collaborate under pressure, and remain steadfast in the pursuit of ambitious, long-term goals.

Balancing continuous athletic training with academic coursework instilled a high level of personal discipline and accountability. As an elected team captain, Emily took responsibility for encouraging teammates during challenging matches, fostering mutual trust during doubles partnerships, and maintaining team morale across consecutive postseason championship runs.

Achievements

Emily's varsity tennis career was marked by consistent competitive success at the regional and state championship levels:

  • Team State Runner-Up — 2021 and 2022
  • State Semifinalist in Doubles — 2021 and 2023
  • Regional Runner-Up in Singles — 2022
  • Regional Runner-Up in Doubles — 2021 and 2023
  • Regional Doubles Champion — 2024
  • Coach's Award Recipient — 2023
  • Five-year varsity starter
  • Elected team captain for senior season

Professional Skills Demonstrated

The habits, responsibilities, and challenges of high-level tennis directly translate into core professional and business strengths:

Leadership

Elected team captain by peers and coaches, providing positive guidance, setting a standard of effort, and supporting teammates during high-stakes competitions.

Teamwork

Building synchronization and trust with doubles partners and contributing to collective team scoring in dual matches and state tournament runs.

Communication

Active and decisive on-court communication with doubles partners, collaborative problem-solving between sets, and open dialogue with coaching staff.

Discipline

Maintaining consistent training routines across five years, practicing drills with focus, and developing mental composure in high-pressure match situations.

Time Management

Successfully managing rigorous practice, match travel, and tournament schedules while maintaining academic excellence and community service commitments.

Working Toward Long-Term Goals

Achieving milestones such as becoming a five-year starter, receiving the Coach's Award in 2023, and capturing the 2024 Regional Doubles Championship was the direct outcome of sustained, multi-year effort. This experience reinforced the value of resilience, patient preparation, and dedication—principles Emily applies directly to her academic studies in Finance at LSU and her future career in business.
